## Why I chose Proton VPN

I use Proton VPN for the privacy guarantee, not just the connection. Proton is a
known, audited no-log provider, and the Linux client is genuinely usable.

## The decider

Trust plus a real native Linux app. Mullvad is great, but Proton's ecosystem
and free tier make it the easiest secure choice for me.

## Signals that sealed it

- Independently audited no-logs policy
- Kill switch and Secure Core servers
- Native Linux client that doesn't feel bolted on
