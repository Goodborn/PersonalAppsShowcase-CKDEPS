## Why I chose KolourPaint

For quick image edits — cropping, drawing an arrow, erasing something, resizing
— GIMP is overkill and slow to open. KolourPaint launches instantly and does
exactly what MS Paint used to, properly.

## The decider

Speed and simplicity. When I need to toss a red circle on a screenshot or resize
an image, it's open, done, and closed in ten seconds.

## Signals that sealed it

- KDE-native, integrates perfectly on Plasma
- Lightweight and instant to launch
- Familiar Paint-style tools without the jank
