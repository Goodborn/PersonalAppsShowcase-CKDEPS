## Why I chose Millennium

The default Steam client feels dated. Millennium is a skinning framework that
lays a modern, clean UI on top of it while the actual client keeps working
normally underneath.

## The decider

It gives Steam the visual overhaul it desperately needs without touching the
functionality — my library, friends, and store all work exactly the same.

## Signals that sealed it

- Open source and actively maintained
- Non-destructive — uninstall and Steam is back to stock
- Lets me theme it to match my desktop
