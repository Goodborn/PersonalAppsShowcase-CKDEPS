## Why I chose OBS

There is no real competition. OBS is the standard for streaming and recording
because it's endlessly flexible — scenes, sources, filters, transitions, and a
plugin ecosystem that covers anything you could dream up.

## Why not SimpleScreenRecorder

SSR is great for "record my screen" and nothing else. OBS gives me:
- Scene composition with layers
- Per-source filters
- Audio mixing with per-source volume levels
- Streaming to Twitch/YouTube with proper encoding settings

## Why not recording via GPU

The GPU screen recorder I also use is extremely fast (see that app) but it's
a simple capture — no compositing, no scenes. OBS is my production tool.
