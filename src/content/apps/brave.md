## Why I chose Brave

I wanted a browser that respects my privacy without making me configure 47 settings
to get there. Brave ships with the Shields system — ad blocking, tracker blocking,
and fingerprinting protection are on by default and they just work.

## The decider

Speed. Pages load noticeably faster than any other browser I used because the ads
never even load in the first place. The ad-blocking pivot on my system browser
changed how the entire web feels.

## Signals that sealed it

- Built on Chromium, so every Chrome extension works
- Wallet and built-in password manager that sync across devices
- Optional crypto stuff is there but completely ignorable
