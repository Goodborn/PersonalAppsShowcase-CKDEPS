## Why I chose Readest

Readest is another take on a modern Linux e-book reader, but built on Tauri so
it stays lightweight while looking truly modern.

## The decider

It reads EPUBs, MOBI, and PDFs in a clean view with excellent typography, and it
feels snappy because it's not dragging a whole browser runtime around.

## Signals that sealed it

- Lightweight and fast Tauri app
- Wide format support in one place
- Clean reading UI that gets out of the way
