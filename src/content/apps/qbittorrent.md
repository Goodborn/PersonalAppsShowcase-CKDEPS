## Why I chose qBittorrent

After uTorrent went downhill with ads and bloatware, I needed something clean and
fully featured. qBittorrent is exactly that — it looks like the old, good uTorrent
and does everything it used to, without the junk.

## What I need from a torrent client

- RSS feed support for automated downloads
- Fine-grained per-torrent speed limits
- Web UI for managing downloads from another device
- Search plugins built in

## Why not the others

Transmission is beautiful but too minimal for my needs. Deluge feels dated.
qBittorrent hits the sweet spot — powerful defaults, sane interface, and all the
advanced features hidden just below the surface when I need them.
