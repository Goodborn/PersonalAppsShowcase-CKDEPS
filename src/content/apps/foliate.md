## Why I chose Foliate

Foliate is the reader that makes reading EPUBs feel like reading real books —
proper typography, comfortable layout, and a clean page-turning experience.

## The decider

The reading experience itself. Type rendering on Foliate is noticeably better
than any other Linux e-book reader I tried, and page turning is smooth.

## Signals that sealed it

- Great default typography, fully adjustable
- Built-in dictionary and Wikipedia lookups
- Position sync via KOReader cloud
