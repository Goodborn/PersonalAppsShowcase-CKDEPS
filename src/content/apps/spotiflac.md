## Why I chose SpotiFLAC

SpotiFLAC lets me grab Spotify tracks in genuinely lossless FLAC by pulling them
from high-res services like Tidal, Qobuz, and Amazon Music instead of Spotify's
lossy streams.

## The decider

Lossless quality. My music sounds meaningfully better, and SpotiFLAC is what
made true FLAC downloads simple enough to actually bother with.

## Signals that sealed it

- Real FLAC, not transcoded MP3s masquerading as lossless
- Supports the major high-res streaming catalogs
- Integrates cleanly with Spotify playlists/links
