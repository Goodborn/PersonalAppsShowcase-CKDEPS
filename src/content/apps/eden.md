## Why I chose Eden

Eden is a free, open-source Nintendo Switch emulator forked from yuzu. It
picks up where that project left off and keeps the emulator alive and
improving, with an active release cadence and a community behind it.

## The decider

Its compatibility and performance were the dealbreakers. Games run well on my
CachyOS / KDE Plasma setup out of the box, and the project keeps getting
better with each stable release.

## Signals that sealed it

- Really works on Linux with good performance
- Open source, so it stays maintained
- Regular stable and nightly builds with PGO performance options
