## Why I chose OpenRGB

I refuse to install three vendor apps to control three brands of RGB hardware.
OpenRGB talks to them all from one interface — and it works on Linux, which the
vendor software almost never does.

## The decider

- One unified control panel for my whole desk
- Syncs with the Lighting Control ecosystem
- Runs headless as a server so my scripts control the glow

## Why not vendor apps

Proprietary RGB software is generally bloated, telemetry-heavy, and Windows-only.
My hardware should glow because I said so, locally, on my machine.
