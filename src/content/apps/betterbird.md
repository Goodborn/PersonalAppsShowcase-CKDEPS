## Why I chose Betterbird

I like Thunderbird, but Betterbird is Thunderbird that actually listens. It
pulls in fixes and improvements upstream keeps ignoring — better conversation
handling, smoother UI, fewer papercuts.

## The decider

It fixes real annoyances I hit daily in Thunderbird without changing the muscle
memory I already built up.

## Signals that sealed it

- Drop-in familiar to any Thunderbird user
- Regular releases with fixes and features
- Actively maintained by focused developers
