## Why I chose Bazaar

Bazaar is a Flatpak-first app store: browse thousands of sandboxed apps from
Flathub and install them with one click. It keeps app management simple and
snappy, without the bloat of a full system store.

## The decider

For installing and updating Flatpaks, Bazaar gives me exactly the tools I need
with a much lighter, cleaner interface than the system defaults.

## Signals that sealed it

- Purpose-built for Flatpak / Flathub
- Minimal, fast, and easy to use
- Sandboxed installs without system-level surprises
