## Why I chose Kdenlive

I edit on Arch Linux and Kdenlive is the only editor that feels native to my
platform. It's not Electron running a web app — it's Qt, it's fast, and it
respects my desktop.

## Kdenlive vs DaVinci Resolve

Resolve is objectively more powerful — color grading, Fusion, all that. But:
- The free version is hobbled in ways that matter
- The download wants the Ubuntu .deb even on Arch
- It's a monster to run for what I need

For most editing work — cutting, transitions, titles, basic effects — Kdenlive
does 90% of the job with 10% of the complexity.

## What I actually use it for

- Cutting stream highlights
- Simple multi-track compositions
- Exporting with hardware-accelerated H.264/H.265
