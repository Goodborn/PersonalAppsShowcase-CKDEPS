## Why I chose Blanket

Blanket plays carefully-sampled ambient sounds — rain, thunder, coffee shop,
waves — and lets me mix them any way I want to tune my focus or my sleep.

## The decider

It's local, open source, and free of the ad-laden "lofi rain" websites. One
click and I have rain + thunder + a warm fire going.

## Signals that sealed it

- Kind, non-cluttered GNOME-style UI
- Customizable sound mix with presets
- Plays local audio, fully offline
