## Why I chose Rufin

Rufin is a gorgeous dedicated music client for my streaming setup. It talks
directly to Jellyfin, Subsonic, and Navidrome servers and gives music a proper,
album-first interface.

## The decider

My music lives on a server, and Rufin is the nicest way to listen to it on
Linux. Cover art, albums, and playlists feel like a real music app, not a web
page.

## Signals that sealed it

- Native support for the servers I already use
- Fast, fluid, modern UI
- Offline sync for when I'm away from the server
