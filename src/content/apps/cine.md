## Why I chose Cine

Cine wraps MPV — the best video playback engine on Linux — in a clean,
distraction-free interface. All the muscle of MPV without needing config files.

## The decider

It gives me MPV's flawless playback quality with an interface I don't have to
tinker with. Subtitles, keyboard controls, and playback all just work.

## Signals that sealed it

- MPV rendering quality under the hood
- Simple, modern, comfortable UI
- Works great with subtitles and local media
