## Why I chose Bolt Launcher

Bolt is a no-nonsense launcher for Old School RuneScape that manages plugins in
the background. No fiddling with plugins on every update — it just handles it.

## The decider

Auto-updating plugins. I never have to think about broken plugin versions after
a game update because Bolt sorts it before I even click Play.

## Signals that sealed it

- Hands-off plugin management
- Also supports launcher configs
- Small, focused program that stays out of the way
