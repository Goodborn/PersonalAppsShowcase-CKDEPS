## Why I chose Namida

Namida is a gorgeous local music and video player. Unlike the clunky old-school
media players, it has a modern, fluid interface that just feels nice to use.

## The decider

It handles both my music and video library in one app that doesn't look like it
was designed in 2005. GPU-accelerated playback keeps everything buttery smooth.

## Signals that sealed it

- Clean, modern UI that matches the rest of my desktop
- Supports both audio and video playback
- Actively developed with a nice roadmap
