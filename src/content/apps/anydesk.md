## Why I chose AnyDesk

AnyDesk is the fastest and lightest remote-desktop tool I've used. It connects
instantly and stays responsive even on less-than-great connections.

## The decider

The performance. Flash over to the machine, do the thing, and it feels local —
no TeamViewer-style lag and constant nagging.

## Signals that sealed it

- Runs on Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, iOS
- Tiny footprint compared to alternatives
- Simple address-based connections, no account required
