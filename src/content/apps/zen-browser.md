## Why I chose Zen

I got it for the vertical tabs — and kept it for everything else. Zen takes
Firefox and gives it the polish I always wanted: split view, workspaces,
compact mode, and a customizable UI that doesn't require reading a manifest
to understand.

## Why it beats Firefox

Firefox is fast and private, but its UI hasn't grown up. Zen gives me:
- Vertical tabs that actually fit my wide monitor
- Split-screen browsing without calling a window manager
- Aesthetic theming that looks like it was designed in 2026, not 2006

## Why it beats Vivaldi

Vivaldi is powerful but bloated — too many settings to breathe. Zen nails the
90% case with beauty and restraint. It is genuinely the first browser that made
me want to use the sidebar.
