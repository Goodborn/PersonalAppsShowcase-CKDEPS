## Why I chose GNOME Disks

It's the quickest way to format a USB stick, make a partition, or check a drive.
The interface is clean and visual in a way that command-line partitioning tools
just aren't.

## The decider

For everyday disk operations it covers everything without needing the power
(and risk) of a low-level partitioner like GParted.

## Signals that sealed it

- Simple, safe defaults for common tasks
- S.M.A.R.T. checks built right in
- Works fine on KDE alongside the Plasma world
