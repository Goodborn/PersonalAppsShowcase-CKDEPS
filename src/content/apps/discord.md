## Why I chose Discord

Honestly? It's where the people are. Every dev community, gaming community, and
semi-organized friend group I'm in lives on Discord. The network effect beats
every technical advantage the alternatives have.

## What I actually get from it

- Servers for communities with roles and channels
- Quality voice chat for my gaming sessions
- Screen sharing with friends
- Deep Linux support on Arch via Flatpak

## Why not the self-hosted alternatives

Matrix/Element is architecturally better — I love that I could self-host — but
the flagship Element client is janky compared to Discord's polish. Technical
superiority doesn't win when the UX and the people both live elsewhere.
