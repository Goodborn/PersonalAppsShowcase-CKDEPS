## Why I chose Upscayl

Upscayl does real AI super-resolution locally and free. Old photos and low-res
screenshots come out dramatically sharper — no cloud service, no watermark.

## The decider

It's local and free where the alternatives charge a subscription. Just point it
at an image, pick a model, and the result is legitimately impressive.

## Signals that sealed it

- Runs entirely on my own hardware
- Open source with an active community
- Multiple models — good for photos and for line-art
