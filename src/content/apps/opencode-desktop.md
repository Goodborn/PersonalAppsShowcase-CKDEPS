## Why I chose OpenCode Desktop

I wanted an AI coding assistant that doesn't pull me into someone's cloud
subscription. OpenCode keeps everything local-first — my API keys, my
configuration, my client — and works right from the terminal I already live in.

## Why not Copilot

Copilot is great if you're all-in on the GitHub/autocomplete workflow, but I
wanted something I could script, something that felt like a peer in my shell
rather than a suggest-box in my editor. OpenCode does that.
