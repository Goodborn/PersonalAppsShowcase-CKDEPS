## Why I chose LibreOffice

I need to open, edit, and export office documents without paying a subscription
or sending everything to a cloud. LibreOffice is genuinely capable — Writer,
Calc, Impress all do real work.

## Why not Microsoft 365

- Subscription pricing to rent software
- The Linux version is still just the web client
- My documents shouldn't live only in their cloud

## Why it wins on Linux

Native performance, perfect deep integration with my file manager, and the
`.odt`/`.ods` formats are cleaner to version-control than `.docx` XML soup.
Still ships the still-build (not rolling) for stability.
