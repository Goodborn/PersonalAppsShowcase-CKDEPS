## Why I chose Popcorn Time

Popcorn Time provides a Netflix-like streaming experience for content that
isn't on any paid service. Pick a movie, hit play, and it streams almost
immediately over BitTorrent.

## The decider

The interface is genuinely polished — posters, cast info, ratings — so it feels
like a premium streaming app rather than a torrent frontend.

## Signals that sealed it

- Instant playback without waiting for a full download
- Slick, catalog-style browsing experience
- Open source and community maintained
