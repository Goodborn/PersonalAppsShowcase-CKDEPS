## Why I chose Cozy

Cozy is the audiobook player I wished existed: a proper library view, cover art
everywhere, speed control, sleep timer, and chapter navigation done right.

## The decider

The library browsing. Finding the next book and jumping chapters feels as
polished as a commercial app, but it's fully local and open source.

## Signals that sealed it

- Chapters, bookmarks and speed control done well
- Syncs play position across devices
- Clean GTK interface that fits on Linux
