## Why I chose AppFlowy

I wanted a Notion-style workspace — databases, pages, embedded content — without
my notes being trapped in a proprietary cloud. AppFlowy is local-first and
open source, so my data lives where I say it does.

## Why not Notion

Notion is genuinely good, but:
- Everything lives in their cloud
- Offline mode is a weak afterthought
- The Linux experience always felt bolted on

## Why not Obsidian for this job

Obsidian is my knowledge base and it's for *thinking*. AppFlowy is my *organizer*
— task boards, databases, structured content. They complement rather than compete.
