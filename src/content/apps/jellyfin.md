## Why I chose Jellyfin

I wanted my movie, show, and music library accessible on everything I own —
without paying a Plex subscription or trusting someone else's cloud. Jellyfin
streams straight from my own machine.

## The decider

It's completely free and self-hosted. My media, my metadata, my users, my
rules. The apps on every platform are good enough that I never miss Plex.

## Signals that sealed it

- Zero cost, fully opens source, no accounts
- First-class streaming to phone, tablet, TV, and desktop
- Transcoding and metadata handled automatically
